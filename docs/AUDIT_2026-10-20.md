also exercise the operator CLI against the disposable Compose database.

Schema v2 is a **fresh pilot baseline**, not an automatic in-place migration of an
existing populated v1 installation. Preserve existing volumes and backups; legacy
audit history needs a reviewed migration, without silently rewriting its hashes.

Pinned direct dependencies are not a fully hashed transitive supply-chain lock.

operation or automatic publication of political allegations. Real OIDC/MFA, external
anchoring, offsite/PITR recovery, legal review, penetration testing and monitored
deployment remain external work and explicit production gates.
