What is verified?

Each record contains a cryptographic digest, retrieval date, exact API URL and locator into the original response. Origin verification and factual corroboration remain distinct.

What is not inferred?

Correlation is not proof of control. Government budget allocations are not bank transfers. Statistical trends do not assign responsibility to individuals.

Operational boundary

This repository is a controlled, single-project pilot. Confidential sources, public allegations and cross-tenant research are intentionally not enabled.
